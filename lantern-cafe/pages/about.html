Lantern

lantern — light.

A lantern is the light left on for whoever's still coming. The word names the room and names the feel. A cafe where you linger, a gallery where you look, a long table where strangers end up talking.

The room

One room, two reasons.

Lantern is a cafe and a working gallery — equal partners, never one decorating the other. The bar makes a properly pulled espresso and a properly steeped mint tea. The back wall hangs a new show every six weeks.

If you arrive for the coffee, you'll end up looking at the work. If you arrive for the work, you'll end up ordering a second cup. Either way, you stay longer than you meant to.

The art

Whose work hangs here.

Local Toronto artists, visiting artists from across the GTA, and the occasional show from further afield. Painting, photography, textile, mixed media. A new show every six weeks — you can apply to show your work on the back wall.
