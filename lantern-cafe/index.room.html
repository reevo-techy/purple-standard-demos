        Lantern

        The room

        Lantern means light

        Two things share one room here: a café and a working gallery, given equal weight. The word Lantern is painted on the far wall. It isn't a slogan. It's the whole idea: a room where people, coffee, and art are alive in the same light.

        Mornings are café-warm. Evenings tip toward the gallery. The wall changes every six weeks, always making space for Toronto's own artists. Come for the coffee; stay because something on the wall stopped you.

        Open call · Toronto artists

        The next wall is yours

        There's always an empty frame in the room. If you make work and you're near Toronto, we want to see it: painting, textile, photography, print, anything that holds a wall for six weeks.

        Send a few images, a line about the work, and roughly how much wall you'd need. We answer every submission.
