Is this right for me?

An implant is often the most natural-feeling way to fill a gap. It may suit you if:

  • You're missing one tooth, several teeth, or want a more stable alternative to a denture.
  • You'd like a solution that doesn't rely on grinding down healthy neighbouring teeth.
  • You want something permanent that you care for just like a natural tooth.
  • Your gums are healthy and there's enough bone to support the implant — we'll check this for you.
