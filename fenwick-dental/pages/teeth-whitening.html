Is this right for me?

Professional whitening goes well beyond a drugstore kit. It's a good fit if:

  • Your teeth have yellowed or dulled from coffee, tea, red wine or time.
  • You want a faster, more even result than over-the-counter strips give.
  • You've an event coming up — a wedding, a milestone — and want to look your best.
  • You'd like it done under professional care, with your gums protected throughout.
