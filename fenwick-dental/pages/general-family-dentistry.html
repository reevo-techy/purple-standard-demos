Is this right for me?

General dentistry is the foundation everything else builds on. It's right for you if:

  • It's time for a routine checkup and cleaning — or it's been a while and you'd like a fresh start.
  • You're booking for children and want a practice that's gentle and patient with first visits.
  • You have a tooth that's sensitive, chipped, or bothering you and want it looked at.
  • You'd like one practice to care for the whole family, from toddlers to grandparents.

What to expect

1

We talk it through

A full but gentle exam — and we explain what we see in plain language, not jargon.

2

We treat what's needed

Cleanings, fillings or crowns done comfortably, with your questions welcome at every step.

3

We keep you on track

A simple plan and a reminder when your next visit is due. That's it.

How long it takes

A checkup and cleaning is usually about 45–60 minutes. [CONFIRM]

How many visits

Routine care is a single visit. Fillings or crowns may need one or two. [CONFIRM]
