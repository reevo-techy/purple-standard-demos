Your first visit, made easy

Whether you're new to Toronto, switching practices, or coming back to the dentist after a long gap — you're welcome here, and there's no judgement at the door.

Three simple steps

How it works

1

Book online or call

Choose a time online in under a minute, or call (416) 555-0134 and we'll find one with you. Mention any language preference and we'll match you with the right team member.

2

Complete your forms

We'll email a short digital intake form. Filling it in beforehand means your first visit starts on time and we already know your history. [CONFIRM intake link]

3

Come in for your cleaning

Meet your clinician, have a gentle cleaning and a full assessment, and leave with a clear, no-pressure plan for anything we find.

Direct billing

We handle the paperwork. We bill most insurers directly and accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan — so you only pay any balance that's left, if any. [CONFIRM direct billing setup & insurers]

What to bring

  • A piece of photo ID.
  • Your dental insurance details, or your CDCP member card if you're enrolled.
  • A list of any medications you take.
  • The name of your previous dentist, if you'd like your records transferred. [CONFIRM]

Bringing the kids?

We love first visits. For young children we keep things short, friendly and unhurried — a "happy visit" to get comfortable in the chair, count teeth, and meet the team. No drama, no fuss.

The Canadian Dental Association suggests a child's first visit by around age one, or within six months of their first tooth. If you're not sure, just ask us. [CONFIRM]

Nervous patients

If the dentist makes you anxious, tell us — we'll go slowly, explain everything first, and stop whenever you need.

It's been years?

That's completely okay. We start gently with an assessment and a plan that's comfortable for you.

Six languages

English, Arabic, Spanish, Tagalog, Urdu and Punjabi — let us know your preference when you book.

Let's get you booked in.
