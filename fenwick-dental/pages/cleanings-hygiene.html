Is this right for me?

A hygiene visit is the simplest way to keep small problems small. It's right for you if:

  • You're due for your regular six-month cleaning. [CONFIRM recall interval]
  • Your gums bleed when you brush, or feel tender or puffy.
  • You've noticed staining, buildup, or that your teeth just don't feel fresh.
  • It's been a long time since your last cleaning and you want a gentle reset.
