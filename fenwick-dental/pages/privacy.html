  • Health information collected as part of providing dental care, kept in your patient record at the practice — not on this website.

How we protect it

We take reasonable administrative, physical and technical steps to safeguard your information. Patient health records are managed under the practice's clinical privacy obligations, in line with Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) and Canada's PIPEDA.
