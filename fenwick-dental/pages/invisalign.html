Is this right for me?

Invisalign suits a wide range of smiles. It may be a great fit if:

  • Your teeth are crowded, gapped, or have shifted over the years.
  • You'd like to straighten your smile discreetly — most people won't notice you're wearing aligners.
  • You want to keep brushing, flossing and eating without brackets in the way.
  • You're an adult or teen who's ready to commit to wearing aligners around 22 hours a day.
