Canadian Dental Care Plan

We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan

The CDCP is a federal program helping eligible Canadian residents without private dental insurance afford the care they need. As the plan expands, more Toronto residents — including seniors, families and newcomers — now qualify. If you're enrolled, you're welcome here.

How CDCP works here

  • 1. Apply & enrol. Apply through Service Canada. Once approved, you'll receive a CDCP member card from Sun Life.
  • 2. Book with us. Call (416) 555-0134 or book online, and let us know you're a CDCP member.
  • 3. Bring your card. We'll bill the plan directly. Any patient co-payment is explained clearly before treatment. [CONFIRM]

Who qualifies for the CDCP

Eligibility is set by the federal government. In general, the plan is for Canadian residents who:

  • Do not have access to private dental insurance.
  • Have an adjusted family net income below the program threshold.
  • Have filed their most recent tax return.

Eligibility rules and covered age groups continue to expand. The official Government of Canada page always has the current details — check your eligibility here.

What the CDCP helps cover

The plan contributes toward a range of routine and necessary dental services, which may include:

  • Examinations, X-rays and diagnostics.
  • Cleanings, scaling and preventive care.
  • Fillings and other restorative treatment.
  • Other services as covered by the plan. [CONFIRM specifics with practice]

Some treatments may involve a co-payment depending on your income tier. We'll always walk you through any cost before we begin.
