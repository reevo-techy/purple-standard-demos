Dental pain has a way of taking over your day. If something hurts, is broken, or doesn't look right, call us first thing — we hold room in the schedule for urgent visits.

When to call us

It's worth a call the same day if you have:

  • A toothache that's keeping you up or won't settle with pain relief.
  • A chipped, cracked or knocked-out tooth.
  • A lost filling or crown, or a sharp edge cutting your tongue or cheek.
  • Swelling in your gum, jaw or face — this one shouldn't wait.

If you're knocked out a tooth, keep it moist (in milk or against your cheek) and call us right away.
