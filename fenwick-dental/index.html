Canadian Dental Care Plan

We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan

The CDCP helps eligible Canadian residents without private dental insurance get the care they need. If you're enrolled — or think you might qualify — you're welcome here. Bring your CDCP member card and we'll take care of the rest.

Check if you're eligible → Book your CDCP visit

Quick guide

  • Who qualifies — Canadian residents without private dental coverage who meet the income threshold.
  • What's covered — cleanings, exams, fillings, X-rays and more. [CONFIRM specifics]
  • How to book — apply through Service Canada, then call us with your member number.
