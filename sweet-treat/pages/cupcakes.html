Cupcakes, from scratch

The everyday joy and the foundation of the whole place — dozens of cupcake flavours, baked fresh in our nut-free kitchen, ready by the each or the box.

What’s in the case

Cupcakes are where Sweet Treat started. Everything is made from scratch that morning — real butter, real vanilla, no shortcuts and, of course, no nuts. Classic flavours are out every day, with rotating specials through the week. [CONFIRM full flavour list]

Order them for

  • Class parties and school treats — a nut-free box everyone can share.
  • Office mornings, showers, and just-because afternoons.
  • Birthday boxes — a mixed dozen so nobody fights over the flavour.
  • Dietary needs — gluten-, dairy- or egg-sensitive and vegan on request. [CONFIRM]

How to order

Single cupcakes are first-come at the counter. For boxes and party quantities, call ahead or send a cake-order enquiry so we can have them boxed and ready for you.
