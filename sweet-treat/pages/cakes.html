The cake the birthday is named after — made to order, decorated around your occasion, and baked in a kitchen the allergy parents in the room can trust.

Cakes for the occasion

Round cakes and sheet cakes, classic flavours and the ones the birthday kid asked for, simple buttercream or a fully decorated showpiece. We start from what you’re celebrating and how many people are coming, and build the cake to fit. [CONFIRM cake range & flavours]

We make them for

  • Birthdays — kids’ themes through to grown-up and understated.
  • Celebrations — graduations, anniversaries, showers, new babies.
  • Office and group cakes — sheet cakes that actually serve the room.
  • Allergy-aware tables — a from-scratch, nut-free cake everyone can eat.

How to order

Send a cake-order enquiry or call the shop. We’ll talk through flavour, size, the look you want and any dietary needs, then confirm a price and a pickup time. A little notice means we can do it properly — for decorated cakes, the earlier the better.
