Our story

A bakery the whole party can eat at

Sweet Treat Desserts is a from-scratch, 100% nut-free bakery in Hackney, London — built so the kid with the allergy gets the same cake as everyone else.

From scratch, and nut-free

Sweet Treat started as a small bakery on Lambton Row with one clear idea: make beautiful cakes and treats the way they should be made — from scratch — in a kitchen that’s completely free of peanuts and tree nuts. [CONFIRM the founding story — the founding year, the family behind it]

For a lot of Hackney families, that’s not a nice-to-have. It’s the difference between their child being part of the party and watching from the side. A nut-free bakery means a birthday cake nobody has to think twice about.

The open kitchen

There’s no “back” here. Stand at the counter and you can watch the bakers ice a cake or pipe a tray of cupcakes — the kitchen is open, on purpose. It’s how you keep standards honest, and it’s a good show with a coffee.

There’s a second Sweet Treat in Walthamstow, too — but this is the Hackney shop, on Lambton Row. [CONFIRM how to present the two locations]
